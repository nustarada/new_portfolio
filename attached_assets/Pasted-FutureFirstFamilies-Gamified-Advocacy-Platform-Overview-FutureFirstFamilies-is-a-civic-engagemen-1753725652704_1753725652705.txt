FutureFirstFamilies – Gamified Advocacy Platform

Overview

FutureFirstFamilies is a civic engagement platform designed to empower parents to take monthly actions on educational reform. I designed and developed the entire platform from the ground up with a gamified, user-friendly approach tailored for non-tech-savvy users.

⸻

Problem

Parents were overwhelmed by traditional advocacy tools that were text-heavy, form-based, and lacked guidance. This resulted in poor engagement, low action completion rates, and overall frustration.

⸻

Goal

To create a lightweight, engaging platform where users could:
	•	Take monthly actions easily
	•	Track their progress through points and milestones
	•	Participate in leaderboards and referral programs
	•	Manage everything from a clean, guided dashboard

⸻

My Role
	•	UX Research
	•	Information Architecture
	•	Wireframing & Prototyping
	•	UI Design & Design System
	•	Full Front-End Development

⸻

Process
	1.	Conducted interviews with advocacy organizers to understand user behavior
	2.	Created user flows and low-fidelity wireframes
	3.	Designed high-fidelity mockups and a reusable design system
	4.	Developed the platform using React in Replit
	5.	Integrated gamification, referrals, and accessibility features
	6.	Tested and refined based on team feedback

⸻

Key Design Decisions
	•	Combined “Monthly Action” and “Activity Hub” into a single tab-based experience
	•	Designed milestone-based reward system for completing actions
	•	Included a referral leaderboard to encourage viral growth
	•	Built a simple admin dashboard for content management
	•	Added accessibility features including light/dark modes and clean typography

⸻

Outcome
	•	✅ 30% increase in completed actions within the first month
	•	✅ 4× higher user retention compared to previous system
	•	✅ 1500+ parents onboarded in the first 3 weeks
	•	✅ Significant improvement in action tracking and admin workflow

⸻

What I Learned

This project reinforced the importance of building for non-tech-savvy users. Simplifying flows, reducing cognitive load, and gamifying progress can meaningfully boost engagement. It also allowed me to handle full design-to-code execution, sharpening both my design system and front-end skills.

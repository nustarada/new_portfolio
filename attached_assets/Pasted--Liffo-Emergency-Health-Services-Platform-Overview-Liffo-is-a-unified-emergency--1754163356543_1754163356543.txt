# Liffo - Emergency Health Services Platform

---

## 🌐 Overview

**Liffo** is a unified emergency health services platform designed to provide fast, reliable access to critical healthcare. It closes the gap between patients and healthcare providers by offering services such as ambulance booking, doctor consultations, lab tests, medicine delivery, and more. The platform empowers users to manage their healthcare needs quickly and efficiently—all in one place.

---

## 🔧 My Role: Lead Product Designer

* Led the end-to-end design process
* Conducted user research, created wireframes, designed UI, and built interactive prototypes
* Collaborated with cross-functional stakeholders to ensure alignment with medical workflows and user needs

---

## ⚡ The Problem

Emergency healthcare access in India is fragmented. Users often rely on multiple disjointed platforms for various services, leading to delays, confusion, and inadequate care—especially in critical situations.

---

## 🌎 The Solution

Liffo simplifies emergency healthcare by offering a comprehensive solution where users can:

* Book ambulances
* Consult with general physicians and specialists
* Schedule lab tests and order medicines
* Access premium services like Elite Doctors, Home Care, and Hospital Appointments
* File insurance claims directly through the platform
* Get real-time help through an AI-powered chatbot

---

## 📅 Timeline

**3 Months (Initial Phase)** + Ongoing iterations based on user feedback and business needs

---

## 🎨 Key Features

### ⚕️ Emergency Services

* Real-time ambulance booking with location tracking
* Optimized routing based on urgency and location

### 👨‍⚕️ Doctor Consultation

* Book consultations with general practitioners and specialists
* Telemedicine support for remote access

### 🧪 Lab Tests & Medicines

* Schedule certified lab tests
* Order medicines with home delivery

### ⭐ Elite Doctors *(New)*

* Premium consultation with top-tier medical experts
* Faster access, exclusive slots, and detailed profiles
* Filters for specialization, hospital affiliations, and user ratings

### 🏡 Home Care *(New)*

Book nurses and caregivers for a variety of in-home services:

* Short-term nursing support
* Post-surgical recovery care
* Critical nursing care
* Chronic disease management
* Physiotherapy and occupational therapy

### 🏥 Hospital Services *(New)*

* Book ambulance rides specifically for hospital transfers
* Schedule in-person doctor appointments at partnered hospitals

### 🧾 Insurance Claim *(New)*

* Direct insurance claim filing and tracking within the app
* Integrated with major healthcare providers
* Reduces paperwork and claim processing time

### 🤖 AI-Powered Chatbot *(New)*

* Instant answers to user queries
* Helps with triage, service navigation, and booking support
* Available 24/7 for critical help

---

## 🤖 Design Approach

* **User Research**: Conducted surveys and interviews across Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities
* **Wireframing**: Transitioned from low-fidelity to high-fidelity wireframes in Figma
* **UI/UX Design**: Focused on clarity, accessibility, and trust using a medical-safe color palette
* **Design System**: Developed scalable, reusable components for rapid development

---

## 📊 Measurable Impact

* Reduced ambulance booking time by **40%**
* Increased repeat consultation rate by **28%**
* Introduction of new services resulted in **2x user engagement** within 2 months

---

## 📖 Key Learnings

* Designing for healthcare requires a balance of empathy, accuracy, and legal compliance
* Simplicity and clarity are crucial, especially during emergencies
* Thoughtful categorization and visual hierarchy reduce user anxiety and improve decision-making
